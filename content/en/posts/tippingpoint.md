## Preface

The possibility of artificial intelligence surpassing humans in the near future is increasingly likely[^1], yet awareness and preparation for its risks remain insufficient.
In this text, I will define AGI as artificial intelligence that can perform all economically meaningful cognitive and physical tasks at a lower cost than any human.
[^1]: The esteemed scientist Sir Demis Hassabis anticipated this around 2030.

I believe the threats from artificial intelligence can be broadly divided into three categories:
- (1) The alignment problem—AI itself acting contrary to humanity's intentions,
- (2) Problems arising from humanity (whether individuals or nations) gaining access to more powerful weapons (in the form of intelligence),
- (3) Socio-structural problems within humanity, including wealth inequality and political systems.

I believe (2) will somehow be resolved (after great chaos and violence), and (1) is at least widely recognized as a problem, with many great scientists working to solve it.
In contrast, I think (3) is one of the most important issues of our time, yet it is not being sufficiently discussed.

If there were a 5% chance that a meteorite capable of extinguishing humanity would hit Earth within 10 years,
we would take various active measures, including international cooperation, to prepare for it.
I believe there is more than a 5% chance of facing an extreme dystopia due to problem (3) within 10 years,
yet on this issue, there is an eerie silence.

Hoping that more people will recognize and discuss what I consider to be problem (3),
I write about this issue and my proposed solution: **KILLSWITCH DEMOCRACY**.

## An Unprecedented Future

Above, I provided a quite strong definition of AGI.
While opinions may differ on how likely it is that we will actually reach AGI meeting this definition in the near future,
this text assumes that possibility is significant and will skip that discussion.

After reaching AGI that satisfies this strong definition, many things we take for granted and unconsciously assume will collapse.
Understanding post-AGI society as merely existing society with various numbers changed is completely wrong.
For example, the following statements are meaningless because the assumptions they make will not be true after AGI:
- Technology has advanced without reducing jobs so far, and it will be the same this time.
- Licensed professions will be fine.
- Those in power will release money because money must circulate for society to function.

To think about post-AGI society, rather than using inductive reasoning from observations we encounter daily,
we must use deductive reasoning from first principles on a completely blank slate.
And in discussing this, whether one was left-wing or right-wing in the old-fashioned socio-political systems of the past is absolutely not important.

## Why Democracy Can Be Sustained

What I fear most about post-AGI society is that democracy as we know it will become difficult to maintain.
Why can democracy be sustained?
Because the general public has **bargaining power**.

In a modern democratic nation, imagine a tyrannical dictator emerges who seeks to maximize their own interests while ignoring the rights of the general public.
In this case, there are various ways the public can resist dictatorship. Particularly, the two most fundamental methods are:

- Strikes
- Armed struggle

In the case of strikes, they damage the national economy, thereby also damaging the tyrant's interests.
In the case of armed struggle, they damage the military force controlled by the dictator, and ultimately, removing all force owned by the dictator can stop the dictatorship.

The fundamental principle behind both methods (and various other secondary methods) is that the general public has **bargaining power**.

## Can Democracy Be Sustained After AGI?

Now, let's consider whether democracy can be maintained and tyrannical dictators prevented in the same way after AGI.

### After AGI

First, if we were to meet AGI with the modern meritocracy and capitalism-based socio-political system remaining largely unchanged **(of course this is a big assumption, and the goal of this text is to make this assumption false)**,
what would society look like?
- As AI becomes more powerful, companies will increasingly fire employees,
and at the end, all employees except the company's final decision-makers (board of directors, etc.) will be fired.
By definition of AGI, since AGI does business management itself better than humans, current professional managers are not exceptions to this firing either.
Wealth inequality will increasingly intensify.
- One tremendous change caused by AI that we have never experienced before is that **overwhelming wealth superiority leads to overwhelming superiority in all other abilities**.
In modern society, overwhelming wealth superiority did not lead to overwhelming superiority in intelligence, insight, execution ability, etc. Thus, great innovators and entrepreneurs continuously emerged from obscurity, they newly acquired wealth, and society could circulate. But AI completely shatters this. Those with more wealth can train stronger models, leading to overwhelming superiority in intelligence, insight, execution ability, etc. Creating new wealth from obscurity becomes impossible, and wealth inequality accelerates.
- After AI, force will also be handled by AI, which is also a tremendous change humanity has never experienced.
One important factor making dictatorship difficult to maintain is that force (soldiers and police) has human hearts.
Because force exists in a form capable of compassion and empathy for fellow humans, factions with stronger justification can persuade the military, and end tyrannical dictatorship through armed revolution.
Now, suppose AI develops purely based on economic incentives as it does now, and compassion for humans is not included in the objective function. [^2]
Then the class monopolizing wealth will possess force without human hearts, and armed revolution in its existing form becomes impossible.

[^2]: Esteemed scientists Ilya Sutskever, Dario Amodei, and others argue that we must create AI with compassion for humans.

### Can Democracy Be Sustained?

If we develop in the current direction and reach AGI,
for the above reasons, wealth polarization will increasingly accelerate, and at the end,
a class monopolizing wealth, intelligence, and force will emerge. Crossing the wall of this class becomes nearly impossible.

In this state, is democracy sustainable? Because there is almost no **bargaining power** remaining for the general public, at this point, democracy becomes nearly impossible.

If humanity becomes nearly useless and democracy cannot be maintained, what kind of life will they live?
- Even in the worst case, for quite a long time, humanity will likely not be exterminated for purposes such as biological experiments, sexual pleasure, relative superiority through comparison, or providing drama.
However, it will be a life similar to livestock.
- In the best case, if the class monopolizing wealth continues to maintain at least minimal compassion,
and if even the crumbs they leave behind are not less than humanity's current total production,
they might live a not-so-bad life. But even in this case, crossing the wall of class will not be permitted. (Similar to the relationship between the Olympian gods and humans)

## Techno-political Tipping Point

For the following discussion, I want to define a new term, **Techno-political Tipping Point**, as follows:
The point at which, no matter what efforts humanity makes, we become trapped in the local extremum described above and can never escape.

I still think there is quite some time before this Techno-political Tipping Point.
Although wealth inequality is already extreme, we are far from a state where an extremely small class monopolizes all wealth.[^3]
Wealth inequality will begin to accelerate around the time of AGI, and if we reach the point where a politically homogeneous small group (probably around 10^4 people) meaningfully owns wealth, that will likely be after the Tipping Point has passed.
